What exactly do I get in the ₹1 audit? ▼
A 30-minute strategic analysis covering your current positioning, competitive gaps, target audience alignment, and a clear roadmap for profile transformation. You'll get actionable insights on headline optimization, about section strategy, and content positioning.
How long does a complete transformation take? ▼
Typically 4-6 weeks from audit to full implementation. That includes profile updates, content calendar setup, and your first 30 days of thought leadership content. Results start showing within 2-3 weeks as algorithm changes propagate.
Do you work with people early in their careers? ▼
Absolutely. We've worked with executives at all career stages—from emerging leaders (5-7 years experience) to C-suite veterans. The principles of positioning and authority-building apply universally; we just scale the strategy to your stage.

The ₹1 audit is designed for exactly this. You'll walk away with clear next steps, a positioning statement, and a prioritized action list. Many clients self-execute on this for 2-3 months before engaging us for acceleration or scaling.

We guarantee that if you implement our recommendations with consistency, your profile visibility and inbound opportunities will improve within 90 days. We measure success by impressions, connection acceptance rates, and inbound message quality—not vanity metrics.

Never. We work through direct feedback, guidance, and strategic recommendations. You retain full control of your profile and execute changes yourself or through your team. Complete transparency and security are non-negotiable.

We monitor LinkedIn's official announcements, run regular A/B tests on client profiles, and track trending positioning strategies across our network of 100+ executives. Every quarterly review includes algorithm insights and tactical adjustments.
